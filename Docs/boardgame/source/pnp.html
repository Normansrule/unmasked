UNMASKED

The Hollow Creek Board Game · print-and-play
Solve the haunting. Unmask the person. 1 to 5 players · ages 10+ · about 45 minutes.

What you need

  • This printout: a 4-sheet board, 85 cards, card backs and a token sheet.
  • 4 pawns for the Solvers and 1 for the Haunter (or use the Solver cards' corners), a six-sided die, about 20 small cubes or coins for Fear.
  • Optional: the Unmasked video game as a companion app. It hides the culprit and plays the Haunter, so nobody has to.

The goal

A Hollow Creek resident is wearing a folklore mask to cover up something ordinary. Find two true clues, build a trap at the case house, catch the Haunter and name who is under the mask before the clock strikes midnight (round 12).

Setup

  1. Pick a case: Pemberton Cottage, the Farm, the Willow Lane Victorian or Blackwood Manor (the Historical Society is the finale, after the other four). Take its case card, 4 suspect cards and its 8 clue tokens.
  2. Pick a Folklore Mask card for the Haunter and shuffle the 6 Haunt cards (reshuffle when they run out).
  3. Hide the culprit. App: it picks. Without the app: one player who will not be a Solver (or a friend) shuffles the 4 suspects and secretly takes one. Its 3 trait icons are the true clues.
  4. Take the 3 true clue tokens, plus 2 other clue tokens from the same house as decoys with a decoy marker hidden under each, plus 3 "nothing here" blanks. Shuffle all 8 face down and lay them on the case house space and the nearest lantern spaces.
  5. Solvers start at Club HQ. The Haunter starts on the case house. Midnight marker on 1.

A round

  1. Solvers each take 2 actions: Move up to 3 spaces · Search (flip a clue token where you stand) · Interview (read a suspect's alibi) · Light a lantern on a lantern space and hang it on the Hook Board · Fetch/Build a trap piece (pieces wait at HQ) · use your ability.
  2. Haunter: flip the top Haunt card and do it, then move 2 spaces toward the nearest Solver. A human Haunter instead spends 3 Ectoplasm on the same powers or on moves.
  3. Clock: move the midnight marker one hour.

Fear

Hauntings add Fear to your Solver card. At 5 you run back to HQ and skip a turn, then drop to 2. Nobody is ever out. Each time someone runs, the board's Fear track rises one; when it reaches the ghost, the Haunter gets away.

Clues and decoys

Every suspect has one unique trait (circled red on their card) and two shared ones, so any two true clues name exactly one suspect. Decoys point the wrong way but can always be disproved: on a lit lantern space, spend an action to lift one found clue. A ghost marker underneath means it was a decoy. You can also check an alibi at the place a suspect names (2 checks a game).

Lanterns

Every 4 lanterns on the Hook Board, all Solvers drop 1 Fear. Light all 12 for a bonus sticker in the campaign.

Catching the Haunter and unmasking

  1. With two true clues found, build the 3 trap pieces on the case house space.
  2. When the Haunter is on or next to the trap, any Solver there may spring it: roll the die. 1 to 4 catches the Haunter (with the Spook die, a lantern or ghost face).
  3. Accuse. Right: read the culprit's motive aloud and win. Wrong: lose an hour and try again. Pemberton allows 3 accusations, the other houses 2.

The Haunter wins at the end of round 12 or when the Fear track fills.

Haunted places

Six haunted places sit around the town: Ravencrag Castle, Cackling Circus, Crowley Tower, Saltmarsh Harbor, Smugglers' Cave and Witchwood Swamp. Each has a card with a small haunting to bust. Busting one earns a Curio token: discard it any time for an extra move, a free lift-check or 1 less Fear.

Modes

  • Solo: play two Solvers against the Haunt deck.
  • Co-op: 2 to 4 Solvers against the Haunt deck or the app.
  • Versus: one player is the Haunter and secretly one of the suspects. They seed the clues, plant up to 2 decoys and spend Ectoplasm. Solvers win by unmasking them.
  • Family: no decoys, 8 rounds.
  • Campaign: play houses 1 to 4, then the Historical Society finale, "The Mask Keeper".

Playing with the video game

Each case card has a code (Pemberton is HC-01-LANTERN). The video game's Board Game Companion screen can run the case for you: it picks the culprit, tells you where clues go, plays the Haunter with sounds and keeps the clock. Codes only add album stickers and fresh setups. Nothing is ever sold.

Printing

Print at 100% on letter paper, card stock if you can. Trim the 4 board sheets on the image edge and tape them in a square (A top-left, B top-right, C bottom-left, D bottom-right). Cards are poker size (2.5 x 3.5 in) and fit standard sleeves; print the backs page if you want matching backs, or sleeve over a playing card.
